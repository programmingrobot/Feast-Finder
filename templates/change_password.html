Ideal Password Suggestion

On first setup, the current password comes from the ADMIN_PASSWORD environment variable.

A strong password is usually easier to protect when it mixes letters, numbers, and special characters.

  • Add special characters such as `!`, `@`, and `#`.
  • Try integrating numbers into the password, such as replacing letters or adding numbers between words.
  • Longer passwords are usually stronger.
  • Avoid names, birthdays, and easy patterns.

Example of a strong password: `River!29Stone#Cloud`
